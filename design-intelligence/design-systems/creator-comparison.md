## 3. Conflicting recommendations (preserved, not averaged)

1. **Price placement**: Saptarshi speech "price visible early" (S10 7:25) vs his own bottom-anchored
   price card (frame-verified) vs Kole mid-card XL price (S01). → Serial-anchor rule: first or
   last, never middle; exact slot = brief's decision point.
2. **Density posture**: Kole deletes (rows, KPIs, dead cards) vs uxpeak adds instruments
   (histograms, counts, median lines). → Baseline minimal, add where a decision needs data.
3. **Icons in marketing**: Kole bans emoji but uses icon cards sparingly; uxpeak replaces icon
   features with real UI. → Icons = UI furniture; marketing sells with product artifacts.
4. **Palette teaching depth**: Saptarshi full theory (harmonies, HSB) vs Kole pragmatic caps
   (60/30/10 + avoid bright AI palettes). → Same conclusion, different altitude; beginners get
   the deck, practitioners get the caps.

## 4. Context-dependent decisions (from the sources)

- Dark vs light: dark is default for dev/AI products (S09 21:18 claim + every Kole demo);
  light editorial when brand sells taste (Saptarshi's own cream slides, Wayfare).
- Skew/motion in hero: fits consumer tools (S01 LinkGuard, S03) — would clash in compliance/
  enterprise (personality dial, S03).
- Exact spacing values: Kole 32/24 cadence vs course 70px hero offset — numbers differ because
  surface differs; the RHYTHM consistency is the transferable part.
