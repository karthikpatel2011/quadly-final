# Pattern: Onboarding & activation (quantified — Mobbin's 1,460-flow study)

Source: video-20 (Mobbin, 22 frame-verified stat/example screens; 986 apps, Apr 2026).
The only source in the library with A/B-measured numbers. Also exchange-type research method
(reference 1,460 real flows before designing).

## Measured landscape (frame-verified stats)
- Average flow = 25 screens; 7 of 10 longest flows are FINANCE apps (Hyundai Card, Lemonade,
  Monzo — verification-heavy domains); Duolingo ≈ 60 screens before sign-up (the engagement
  exception).
- Pattern prevalence: **Verification 43.9%** · Personalization 23.4% (AI apps only 7%) ·
  Paywall 22% · Social seeding 9.4% · Tutorial 7.9% · Connecting accounts 6.9%.
- Web onboarding is 21% shorter than iOS.

## Measured A/B wins (balance argument, not vibes)
| Change | Result |
|---|---|
| Headspace: multi-select goals | +10% trial conversion |
| Dollar Shave Club: conversational copy | +5% subscriptions |
| Grammarly: quiz-tailored pricing | ~+20% upgrades |
| Mural: 6-step checklist | +10% relative 1-week retention |
| Houzz: split form (scopes a scary form) | +15% conversions |

## Framework (creator's): Signup → Setup → Aha Moment → Habit
- Signup: shortest legal path; defer verification; social seeding optional.
- Setup: multi-select personalization (cheap, measured win); quiz answers may tailor pricing.
- Aha: burn the tutorial (7.9% prevalence — most products don't need one); show the product
  working with seeded/example data if real data is missing; "game empty state" (Mercury) and
  founder notes are trust plays.
- Habit: coined metrics (Duolingo streaks, Apple rings, Oura) + recaps/briefs (Origin, Runna)
  manufacture daily reasons to return.

## Design-rules derived (`interp` consolidation, each measured half is cited above)
1. Ask multi-select questions, not free-text, in setup.
2. Tailor pricing pages from quiz/graph data when you have it (≈+20% is the biggest lever).
3. Split long forms by commitment boundary ("create account" vs "billing").
4. Never let the flow be verification-first unless your domain forces it.
5. Empty states are onboarding — seeded content ≥ blank canvas.
6. Ship the confirmation/celebration moment (video-02's success micro-interaction applies).

## Inside dashboards (companion study, video-13)
Charts appear in 45.6% of dashboard apps; 19% make the dashboard the home screen; AI/CRM
categories get ~2× saves+exports (avg 293) — i.e., dashboards must DO something (export, save)
not just display. Winning devices: insights-beside-charts, recap/brief headers, ONE deliberate
visual device (Shopify globe, Front heatmap), story-format data (Opal).
