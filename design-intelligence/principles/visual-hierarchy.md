# Visual Hierarchy — consolidated principles

Sources: S10 (traffic-control, von Restorff, serial position), S01 (price/name swap, row
reading line), S06/S07 (trust adjacency, decision-support), S03 (radial hierarchy).

## Rules
- **One figure per screen section**: decide the single element that must win; exaggerate via
  size XOR weight XOR color; demote the rest. Emphasis multiplies → effect divides. [G]
- **Contrast in ≥2 dimensions** (size+weight+color) survives color-blindness and bad screens. [G]
- **Reading line in rows**: identity → status → meta → actions; counts right-aligned. [C: S01, adopted as R]
- **Pricing inversion**: plan NAME small (nobody cares), PRICE huge (everybody cares); discount
  visible; next-tier delta explicit. "No one cares about that." [G: S01 4:14]
- **Serial positioning**: context first, support middle, decision last; onboarding/checkout/long
  pages especially. Frame-verified price-as-bottom-anchor tension with speech — anchor rule. [R]
- **Trust adjacency**: rating/proof sits directly beside its claim; testimonial avatars above
  headline (S03 reference frame). [R]
- **Radial emphasis for showcases**: hero artifact visibly louder (scale/elevation) than
  satellite items. [C: S03]
- **Chunking**: headed blocks ≤6-word titles; "chunking is spacing at a macro level". [G]
- **Dedup rule**: a stat/module appears once, where its decision happens — three repeated KPI
  strips = AI tell. [G]
